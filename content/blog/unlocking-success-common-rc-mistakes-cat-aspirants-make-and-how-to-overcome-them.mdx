## The Hidden Trap of Misalignment

When diving into the world of reading comprehension (RC) for the CAT exam, many aspirants fall into a familiar rhythm. They pick passages, skim through the words, and feel a false sense of confidence as they gulp down information. The emotional high of completion can be misleading. Too often, it leads to overlooked details and subtle nuances that are crucial for accuracy. 

But beneath the surface, anxiety simmers. The fear of underperformance breeds a cycle of frustration, and mistakes compound with each careless reading. You’re entrenched in a cycle but unaware of the underlying pitfalls that threaten your path to success.

![Thinking](/blog/IM-18.png)

---

## What You Think You Are Doing

You might believe you're doing just fine by reading swiftly and answering the questions. You're convinced that speed will give you the edge, enabling you to tackle more questions within the tight deadlines of the exam. It feels like you’re making progress, but at what cost?

---

## What You Should Be Doing Instead

- **Focus on Understanding:** Read each passage with intent. Aim to grasp the main idea and supporting details rather than racing through the text.
- **Slow Down on Questions:** Take a moment to dissect the questions. Understand what they are truly asking rather than jumping to conclusions.
- **Revisit Passages:** Don’t hesitate to re-check the passage when a question stumps you; you’ll often find the answer hidden within.

---

## Where the Mistake Actually Happens

![Decision making](/blog/image2.png)

The critical error lies in the assumption that reading quickly equates to better performance. Speed reading can lead to misinterpretations. It isn’t just about the words; it’s about connecting ideas and grasping subtle meanings. This dissonance in comprehension leads to miscalculated answers, ultimately sabotaging your accuracy.

---

## The Discipline You Need

Discipline is the cornerstone of accuracy in RC. Embrace a reading routine where you prioritize understanding over speed. Make it a point to practice with a critical eye, and gauge your comprehension before moving on. 

---

## The Role of Elimination

Elimination is not just a technique; it’s a mindset. Approach each question with an analytical lens. Dismiss the options that don’t resonate with your understanding of the passage. By narrowing down your choices, you enhance the likelihood of landing on the correct answer. It’s a powerful tool that often makes the difference between a correct and incorrect response.

---

## Final Thought

Success in the CAT exam does not hinge on racing against time but on cultivating a culture of accuracy and discernment. The journey through reading comprehension is not merely about answering questions but about nurturing the skill to decode complexity and find clarity amidst chaos. Remain steadfast, and allow each passage to unravel its truths; accuracy will follow.
