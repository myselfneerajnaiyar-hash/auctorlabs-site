## Speed: The Key to Alliance in Verbal Ability 

As CAT aspirants, you often find yourselves racing against the clock. Reading Comprehension (RC) can be grueling, especially when every second counts. The pressure mounts, yet what you must remember is this: speed isn’t just about fast reading; it’s about efficient understanding. The art of quick comprehension is your strategic ally in mastering CAT verbal ability. If you can decode people’s thoughts rapidly and accurately, you own the test.

Imagine plowing through dense paragraphs, every word holding a potential clue to unlock the questions. Each line is saturated with hidden meanings waiting for your timely insight. The thrill of connecting dots at breakneck pace is what sets top-scoring aspirants apart from the rest. This mastery over speed, when wedded to accuracy, transforms you into an unstoppable force.

![Thinking](/blog/IM-19.png)

---

## What You Think You Are Doing

Most aspirants mistakenly think that skimming is synonymous with speed. They zip through passages, chasing time, while missing integral parts of the text. This hasty approach not only hampers comprehension but also blinds you to critical details, leading to costly errors. You may feel that you’re optimizing your time, but what you’re actually doing is creating a false sense of security around your abilities.

---

## What You Should Be Doing Instead

- Focus on key ideas instead of every word; practice active reading.
- Develop a strategic approach to question types; understand what each question is really asking.
- Time yourself during practice; aim to increase speed without sacrificing comprehension.

---

## Where the Mistake Actually Happens

![Decision making](/blog/IM-10.png)

The core of your mistakes often lies in the initial reading phase. If you don’t prioritize understanding the passage's structure and main ideas, you’ll fall into the trap of guessing on questions. It’s in this essential moment—when you decide how to read—that speed and information begin to diverge. Many assume that rapid reading will automatically yield understanding, but the truth is, it often leads to misplaced confidence.

---

## The Discipline You Need

Speed requires discipline. You must train yourself to recognize and eliminate distractions that derail focus. Limit your rereading; instead, practice summarizing ideas quickly after reading a paragraph or two. Focused [reading speed drills for CAT](/features/speed-drills) can help you work on pace without confusing rushed reading with efficient understanding. Cultivate an environment where you can replicate exam conditions. The more you tune your mind to this speed-accuracy synergy, the sharper your edge will become when it counts.

---

## The Role of Elimination

In CAT’s RC, mastery of elimination is your tactical weapon. Get into the habit of dismissing options that don’t align with your comprehension. Often, the trickiest choices are designed to lure you into a trap. By honing your ability to swiftly identify and discard wrong answers, you free up mental bandwidth to invest in the probable options, enhancing your performance under pressure.

---

## Final Thought

Mastering speed in Reading Comprehension is an art that bridges understanding with time management. Treat each reading passage like a puzzle waiting to be solved, and remember that efficiency is built through consistent, focused practice. As you learn to maneuver through dense texts and sharp interrogatives, you will not just conquer the RC section—you will emerge as a formidable candidate ready to take on the entire CAT challenge.
